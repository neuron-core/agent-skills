# Release readiness

Use the risk-review skill to assess the proposed release and the
rollback-plan skill to establish how the team would recover from failure.

Bring both results into a release recommendation:
- Ready to ship.
- Ready once the stated conditions are met.
- Not ready to ship.

Explain the conditions behind the recommendation and identify any
unresolved risk that the release owner must accept.
